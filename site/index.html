Your documents, reflected perfectly.

A free PDF, image and 3D viewer and editor for Windows 11, inspired by macOS Preview. Mark up, sign, redact and reorganise PDFs, touch up photos and turn 3D models in your hands — in one small, fast window that never goes online.

Everything you open, in one place

PDFs that stay sharp

Continuous, single-page and two-page views, thumbnails, table of contents, bookmarks, search, slideshow and a magnifier. A typical PDF opens in about a quarter of a second.

Markup that stays editable

Highlight, sketch, draw, add shapes, text boxes, notes and signatures. Annotations are standard PDF annotations you can change after saving.

Redaction that really removes

Redacted text, images and graphics are removed from the saved file — not covered with a black box that can be lifted.

Forms, signatures, pages

Fill in forms and sign them. Rotate, reorder, delete, insert, combine and crop pages, or export with effects, a password or a smaller size.

Photos, too

PNG, JPEG, HEIC, WebP, TIFF and more. Crop, resize, adjust colour, remove a background colour with Instant Alpha, and strip location data.

3D models

STL, OBJ, PLY and glTF. Orbit, pan and zoom, fix the orientation, and export to another format or an image.

Made for Windows 11

Light and dark themes that follow Windows, your accent colour, Windows 11's typeface, Print and Share, and a title bar that blends into the toolbar.

Touch and pen work naturally: one finger scrolls, two fingers pinch to zoom, and in a markup tool your finger or pen draws. Narrator can read every control and the text of every page.

Private by design

Paper Mirror makes no network requests at all — no telemetry, update checks or crash reports — and runs no background services.

Before it first saves over a file it keeps the original, and it treats every file as untrusted. Read the privacy and security notes.

Questions

Is Paper Mirror free?

Yes — free and open source under the MIT licence, for personal and commercial use.

Why does Windows warn me when I run the installer?

Releases aren't code-signed yet, so SmartScreen may say it protected your PC. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Every release lists SHA-256 checksums so you can verify the download.

Can I make it my default PDF viewer?

Yes: Help > Use Paper Mirror for PDFs and Images…, then choose the file types in Settings > Default apps.

Does it need administrator rights?

No. It installs for your account only, or runs from a portable zip.

Is it made by Apple?

No. Paper Mirror is independent and isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Apple.
