# Code of Conduct

Paper Mirror follows the
[Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and constructive, assume good intent, and make the project
welcoming to everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity. Harassment,
personal attacks and discriminatory language aren't accepted in issues, pull requests
or any other project space.

## Reporting
To report unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through
[GitHub](https://github.com/rvenning). Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer may
remove contributions, or block people who break this code, as the Contributor Covenant's
enforcement guidelines describe.
